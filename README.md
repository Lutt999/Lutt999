## Research

**ECG models for cardiac-risk prediction** · Ziad Obermeyer Lab, UC Berkeley School of Public Health  
Fine-tuning ECG waveform models, training logistic-regression baselines on clinical features and designing feature-ablation experiments. Completed inference on 800,035 MIMIC-IV ECG records and built workflows linking model outputs to echocardiography and longitudinal hospital data.

**Cuneiform recognition and transcription** · TokenWorks LLC, UC Berkeley Data Discovery  
Designed and lead *From Signs to Sequences*, on how separately available sign images and corpus text can support recognition when paired line annotations are scarce. Built 387,315 synthetic image–sequence pairs and trained CTC and vision-language recognizers.

**Sanger sequencing software and methods** · UC Berkeley DNA Sequencing Facility  
Developing an alternative to the facility's PeakTrace workflow under the supervision of Dr. Scott Geller: AB1 trace diagnostics, reference-based sequence scoring and a machine-learning re-basecaller.
